# Chatfront privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Chatfront lets small shops sell through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and a web chat. This
policy says what data Chatfront keeps, why, and how to have it removed. Contact:
ahsaanmunir53@gmail.com.

## What we keep

- **Messages** a customer sends to a shop that uses Chatfront, and the shop's replies, so the
  shop can answer and take orders.
- **Order details** the customer gives: items, delivery city and address, and how they paid.
- **Phone numbers and account ids** from WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger, so replies reach the
  right person. Where we only need to recognise a number, we keep a one-way hash of it.
- **Pictures, voice notes and payment screenshots** a customer sends, stored privately and
  shown only to that shop's staff through short-lived links.

We do not sell data, show ads, or share one shop's customers with another shop.

## Who processes it

- **Meta** (WhatsApp Cloud API, Instagram Messaging, Messenger Platform) delivers the messages.
- **AI model providers** (Muse Spark Open on Baseten, and Glimmer where configured) read a message
  to draft a reply or read a picture. Code, not the model, decides anything about money, stock
  or prices.
- **Hosting**: Render (app), Neon (database), Supabase (file storage).

## How long

Data is kept while the shop uses Chatfront. Demo shops and their copies are deleted
automatically.

## Your rights and data deletion

A customer can ask for a copy of their data or for its deletion in the chat ("my data"), or by
writing to the shop or to ahsaanmunir53@gmail.com. Deletion removes the person's name, phone
links, messages and media; amounts stay in the shop's books without the person's identity, as
accounting requires.

## Changes

We will update this page if what we keep changes.
